# PhD research questions

## RQ1 — scalable formal verification under cyber uncertainty

How does reachable-set conservatism and runtime scale as attack/fault uncertainty, horizon, and state dimension increase?

## RQ2 — representation choice

When do zonotopes materially outperform interval boxes for cyber-resilience verification, and when does their generator cost dominate?

## RQ3 — simulation versus proof

How often do trajectory-based stress tests miss uncertainty regions that are exposed by sound reachable-set analysis?

## RQ4 — hybrid recovery logic

Can resilient supervisory mode transitions be verified without excessive branch explosion?

## RQ5 — trustworthy digital-twin tightening

Can online digital-twin evidence shrink initial/uncertainty sets while preserving explicit coverage guarantees, thereby reducing formal conservatism?

## RQ6 — learning-enabled controllers

How can neural/learning-enabled control policies be enclosed or abstracted so closed-loop CPS safety remains formally checkable?
