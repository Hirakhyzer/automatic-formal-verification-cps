# Research roadmap

## v0.1 — auditable affine reachability

- outward-rounded boxes;
- zonotopes;
- affine discrete-time systems;
- hybrid branching with box guards;
- unsafe-box properties;
- bounded cyber/fault effects;
- four domain adapters;
- sampling comparison;
- disturbance-margin sweep.

## v0.2 — validated continuous-time linear systems

- interval/zonotope matrix-exponential enclosure;
- time-interval flowpipes;
- support-function properties;
- refinement when an unsafe intersection is spurious.

## v0.3 — nonlinear systems

- Taylor-model or interval remainder bounds;
- adaptive time stepping;
- benchmark against established reachability tools.

## v0.4 — hybrid resilience

- scalable guard intersection;
- mode merging/refinement;
- recovery-controller verification;
- temporal safety specifications.

## v0.5 — digital-twin-guided formal verification

- calibrated online state-set contraction;
- formally justified uncertainty updates;
- model-mismatch monitors;
- proof invalidation when calibration assumptions fail.

## v0.6 — learning-enabled CPS

- neural controller abstractions;
- closed-loop reachable-set analysis;
- comparison with dedicated neural verification frameworks.
